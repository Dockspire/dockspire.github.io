# Dockspire: We ship. Mostly.

A small digital workshop with a slightly unreliable cargo ship. The joke belongs to the illustrated ship; the studio's services remain clear. No people are shown in distress.

References inspected: Brooke Condolora's Bow Waves Animation (https://dribbble.com/shots/3036201-Bow-Waves-Animation), Ebin Sebastian's Boat Website Animation (https://dribbble.com/shots/23092793-Boat-Website-Animation) and Lusion (https://lusion.co/). These informed the layered water, scene-led interaction and visual pacing. Their artwork and website source are not reused.

Original direction: warm ivory, ink, orange-red, sea green and acid yellow; oversized editorial type; a harbor-control readout; a cargo boat built from layered SVG shapes. Containers carry DESIGN, CODE and COFFEE. The last is structurally critical.

The visitor can trigger a docking incident. The bow hits the dock with a comic CRUNCH burst, a short jolt, 22 flying fragments and a large splash. The jagged hull breaks into two independently rotating halves, all three containers fly along separate arcs, and both halves sink. The coffee crate resurfaces with a seagull, a hull fragment and a 404 tag. There is no rescue ring, trailing rope or decorative arrow. Status messages follow the impact, breakup and coffee rescue. Ship it again reverses the breakup to reassemble the vessel. Scrolling also changes the approach to the dock.

Motion is pausable, disabled by default when reduced motion is requested, and suspended when the tab is hidden. Audio is off until explicitly enabled. The main navigation, contact dialog, service content and GitHub links remain usable without the animated sequence.

Fonts: Space Grotesk and IBM Plex Mono from the official Google Fonts repository, self-hosted with OFL licenses.
